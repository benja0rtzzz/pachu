# The Solar System — Mr. Garcia's Class

We learned about the solar system today. Here are the things to remember!

## The Sun

The Sun is a star. It is in the middle of our solar system. The Sun is really, really
big. You could fit one million Earths inside the Sun! The Sun is hot. It gives us
light and warmth. Without the Sun, plants could not grow.

## Planets

There are eight planets that go around the Sun. They go around in circles. We call
each circle an orbit.

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun. It is small and rocky. A day on Mercury is
super long, but a year on Mercury is short.

Venus is the second planet. Venus is the hottest planet because it has thick clouds
that trap the heat. It is sometimes called Earth's twin because it is almost the same
size as Earth.

Earth is our home. Earth is the only planet we know that has life on it. Earth has
water and air, which is why plants and animals can live here. We have one moon.

Mars is the red planet. Mars is smaller than Earth. People want to send astronauts to
Mars one day. Mars has the biggest mountain in the solar system. Its name is Olympus
Mons.

Jupiter is the biggest planet. Jupiter is so big that you could fit all the other
planets inside it. Jupiter has a giant red storm that has been spinning for hundreds
of years. We call it the Great Red Spot.

Saturn is the planet with the rings. The rings are made of ice and rock. The rings
are very pretty when you look through a telescope.

Uranus is a blue-green planet. Uranus is tipped on its side, which is silly. Nobody
knows for sure why.

Neptune is the farthest planet. Neptune is windy and cold. Neptune was found by
math, before anyone saw it through a telescope!

## Moons

A moon is something that goes around a planet. Earth has one moon. Jupiter has many
moons — more than seventy! Some moons are tiny, and some moons are bigger than the
planet Mercury.

## Asteroids and comets

An asteroid is a chunk of rock that goes around the Sun, but is too small to be a
planet. Many asteroids are between Mars and Jupiter, in a place called the asteroid
belt.

A comet is a ball of ice and dust that goes around the Sun. When a comet gets close
to the Sun, the ice melts and you can see a long, glowing tail behind it.

## What I want to remember

The Sun is a star, not a planet. Earth is the third planet from the Sun. Jupiter is
the biggest. Saturn has rings. Mars is red. Comets have tails.
